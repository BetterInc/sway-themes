Wallpaper: green digital rain, 4K, 30s:
  https://pixabay.com/videos/matrix-computer-network-47802/  (Pixabay Content License)
Save as ~/.config/sway-themes/wallpapers/matrix.mp4 (or this directory).

Optional - make the loop seam invisible (blends the last second into the first):
  D=$(ffprobe -v error -show_entries format=duration -of csv=p=0 in.mp4)
  ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -filter_complex "[0:v]split[b][p];[p]trim=0:1,setpts=PTS-STARTPTS[f];[b]trim=start=1,setpts=PTS-STARTPTS[m];[m][f]xfade=transition=fade:duration=1:offset=$(echo "$D-2" | bc)" -an matrix.mp4

Without a video, the theme falls back to wallpaper-still.png at all times.
